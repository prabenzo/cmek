## Overview

**What this milestone accomplishes.**

- The submission exists. The live system becomes something a reviewer can be handed: a short written rationale drawn from the spec (the two promises, the key lease as the one knob, down versus revoked, isolation, the decision log D1–D9, what to do with more time), one screen recording of about five minutes, an index of raw AI transcripts, the finished time log, and a README that links all of it.
- The rationale tells the truth about what was built: every parameter number in it is the value as built rather than the spec table, and where the two differ the sentence reads "spec X, built Y, why"; every cut an earlier milestone took (the D9 always-on fallback, tenant detail, the per-tenant fetch cap, fair-share shedding, two charts, hover and pin, the L1 and L4 lights) is substituted into both the doc and the video script from the time log, so the doc never claims what the demo cannot show.

The only Go change is the pre-agreed one-line flag strike if M5's every-scenario log shows a red L1 or L4, redeployed during the video upload wait and recorded as M5's cut taken by flag.
